What is Early Fatty Liver?

Fatty liver disease occurs when excess fat builds up inside liver cells. In its early stages, it causes zero noticeable symptoms. When detected early, it is 100% reversible with simple nutrition and exercise changes before permanent damage (fibrosis) occurs!
