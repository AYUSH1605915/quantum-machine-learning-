QUANTUMMED • RESEARCH SCREENING PLATFORM

Hybrid Quantum ML for
Neurodegenerative Screening

QuantumMed is a modular research-screening platform combining voice, gait, handwriting and resting-state EEG. The voice pathway contains the quantum core (Random Forest + VQC + fidelity-kernel QSVM), while gait, handwriting and EEG use independently validated classical branch models. Their outputs are combined by validation-weighted late fusion into an explainable Multimodal Pattern Index.

SYSTEM AT A GLANCE

Four signal pathways, one auditable screening workflow

Each modality is processed by its own validated branch. The platform keeps the voice/QML pathway inspectable while combining independent branch outputs only at decision level.

01

Biomedical inputs

Voice, gait, handwriting and resting-state EEG are handled as separate signal pathways with modality-specific derived features.

Diagnosis/status labels are never entered as inference features.
02

Branch models

Voice uses RF + VQC + fidelity-kernel QSVM; gait uses RBF-SVM; handwriting uses Random Forest; EEG uses Logistic Regression.

The quantum circuit is concentrated in the compact voice representation.
03

Late fusion

Available branch scores are weighted by research validation ROC-AUC above chance and renormalized before producing the Multimodal Pattern Index.

The fused index is not a calibrated disease probability.
04

Explainable output

Review the overall pattern index together with modality agreement, branch scores, fusion weights and the detailed voice/QML evidence.

Research decision support only — never a standalone diagnosis.

HOW IT WOULD BE USED

From biomedical signals to qualified review

A supervised research workflow connects available signal measurements, modality-specific processing, branch inference, late fusion and qualified review.

1Acquire

Collect any supported measurement: sustained voice features, gait sensor data, handwriting dynamics or resting-state EEG.

2Process

Apply modality-specific feature extraction and training-fitted preprocessing without injecting diagnostic labels.

3Assess & fuse

Run the appropriate branch models and combine available outputs with validation-weighted late fusion.

4Review

Interpret the pattern index, agreement and branch contributions as research decision support and escalate when appropriate.
